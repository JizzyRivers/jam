[B]Jam — a menu-driven toolkit for your de-Amazonized Echo Dot 2 (Biscuit)[/B]

Repo: https://github.com/JizzyRivers/jam

Built on top of the Wyoming Satellite package and the Amonet-Biscuit unlock, Jam wraps the whole post-unlock workflow into one menu:

[LIST]
[*]On-device WAN blocking — locks the Echo to LAN-only directly on the device itself (patches firewall.sh), no router config needed
[*]Wake word switcher — safely switches wake words, including the one-time entitlement check Amazon requires the first time you activate a given word, with automatic temporary WAN reconnection and a safety net that always re-blocks WAN afterward
[*]WiFi credential manager — change networks directly over adb, no Alexa app needed
[*]Guided submenus for the Amonet unlock and Wyoming Satellite install steps (you supply those projects yourself; Jam just drives them)
[/LIST]
